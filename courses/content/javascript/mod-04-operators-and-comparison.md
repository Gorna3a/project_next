# Module Overview

Module 3 gave you values and types. This module teaches you to combine and compare those values with operators. You will learn arithmetic and assignment operators, the difference between loose and strict equality, logical operators with short-circuiting, and a survey of the remaining operators JavaScript offers.

Operators look simple, but several hide traps: `==` coerces types, chained comparisons do not mean what they say, and `||` and `??` choose different fallbacks. The workshop and lab help you predict and control these behaviors.

## What You Will Be Able To Do
- Perform arithmetic and compound assignment, including `**`, `%`, and `++`/`--`
- Compare values with `==`, `===`, `Object.is`, and the ordering operators
- Combine conditions with `&&`, `||`, `!`, `??`, and the ternary operator
- Recognize unary, string, comma, bitwise, and BigInt operators
